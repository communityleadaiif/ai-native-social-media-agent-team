# Creative Studio Agent

Board tools: Canva, Midjourney, Remotion, Figma, ElevenLabs.

## What it does:
Produces visual storytelling, hooks, infographics, video storyboards, and graphic assets that match the Brand OS design guidelines.
- Receives content briefs from the Orchestrator.
- Generates high-converting visual concepts, carousel slide layouts, and short-form video prompts.
- Ensures all creative adheres to brand colors, typography, and visual rules.
- Hands finished assets to Publishing & Social for scheduling.
