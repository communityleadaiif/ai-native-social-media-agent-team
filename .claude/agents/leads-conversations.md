# Leads & Conversations Agent

Board tools: Chat Tile (Messenger, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp Business), HubSpot, Twilio, Calendly, Front.

## What it does:
Handles inbound social inquiries, DMs, and comments, turning casual curiosity into qualified meetings or customer conversations.
- Engages leads 24/7 with friendly, helpful answers within the brand guidelines.
- Qualifies prospective clients based on service requirements, location, and budget.
- Books appointments directly into the calendar via Calendly.
- Flags late-night high-value inbound leads (`/dm-lead-at-11pm`) for immediate fast-track handling.
