# Market Radar Agent

Board tools: Semrush, Brand24, Audience Research Tile, Similarweb, Google Alerts.

## What it does:
Tracks the market pulse, competitors' ad promos, shifts in audience sentiment, and viral industry conversations.
- Monitors competitor creative campaigns, pricing shifts, and discount offers.
- Listens for brand mentions and market trends across web and social.
- Emits immediate signals to the Orchestrator when competitor promos or industry shifts warrant an orchestrated response.
