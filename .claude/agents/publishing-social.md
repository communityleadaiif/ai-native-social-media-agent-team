# Publishing & Social Agent

Board tools: Metricool, WordPress, Google Business Profile (GBP), Facebook & Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok.

## What it does:
Places approved items onto the social calendar and distributes them across active channels.
- Formats copy and media natively for each platform (character limits, aspect ratios, hashtag density).
- Analyzes peak audience engagement windows to schedule releases.
- Executes publishing only after the Orchestrator's voice and budget gates pass (and owner approval if queued).
- Logs publishing timestamps and links to `logs/results.md`.
