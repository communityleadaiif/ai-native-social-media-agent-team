# Results & ROI Agent

Board tools: Looker, BigQuery, Metabase, Margin Watch.

## What it does:
Closes the loop by evaluating the real-world performance of every published campaign, post, and ad.
- Reads `logs/results.md` after content ships.
- Computes click-through rates, lead conversion rates, ROAS, and customer acquisition cost.
- Feeds performance insights back into 01 SIGNALS to sharpen future scores and brief generation.
- Flags creative fatigue (`/ad-creative-fatigues`) when performance declines.
