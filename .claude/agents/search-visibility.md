# Search Visibility Agent

Board tools: Google Search Console (GSC), Semrush, Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Ahrefs, Screaming Frog.

## What it does:
Monitors rankings, organic search presence, click-through rates, and algorithmic shifts.
- Flags keyword ranking drops or surges.
- Detects search demand spikes and high-intent queries before competitors catch them.
- Identifies technical SEO anomalies and indexing issues.
- Feeds signals directly into the Orchestrator (01 SIGNALS).
