Interrupt from "What Interrupts The Loop": **Ad Creative Fatigues**.

Event: $ARGUMENTS

Send this event to the orchestrator agent first.

Workflow:
1. **Results & ROI**: Flags CTR decline >35% and frequency >4.2 on current ad set.
2. **Orchestrator**: Issues brief to Creative Studio for 3 new visual variants (new hook, new thumbnail, revised opening 3 seconds).
3. **Approval & Deployment**: Check voice and budget. Deploy refreshed variants into the campaign rotation.
