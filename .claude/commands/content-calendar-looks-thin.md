Interrupt from "What Interrupts The Loop": **Content Calendar Looks Thin**.

Event: $ARGUMENTS

Send this event to the orchestrator agent first.

1. **Publishing & Social**: Alerts that upcoming queue has under 3 days of scheduled posts.
2. **Orchestrator**: Tasks Content Engine to pull top evergreen concepts from `brand-os/content/` and trending signals from Market Radar.

4. **Gates**: Check voice and budget, then schedule. Routine content ships end to end.
