# Tools & Integrations Directory

This document details all board tiles, external platforms, and API connectors used by the 8 departments and the Orchestrator.

## Department Tool Integrations

### 1. Search Visibility
- **Google Search Console (GSC)**: Real-time keyword impressions, click trends, query position monitoring.
- **Semrush / Ahrefs**: Competitive organic research, backlink monitoring, volume jump triggers.
- **GA4**: On-site organic landing page session analytics.
- **Screaming Frog**: Automated technical audit crawls for broken links and metadata errors.

### 2. Market Radar
- **Brand24**: Real-time web and social listening for brand mentions and sentiment shifts.
- **Similarweb**: Digital competitor traffic benchmarking.
- **Google Alerts**: Industry news and keyword surge detection.

### 3. Content Engine
- **Claude / LLM Engine**: Core drafting, hook generation, editorial rewriting.
- **Surfer SEO / Frase**: Content brief scoring and semantic NLP term recommendations.
- **Grammarly**: Readability, syntax, and clarity validation.
- **Airtable**: Central content calendar database and production status tracking.

### 4. Creative Studio
- **Canva / Figma**: Graphic layout templates, typography, and carousel card rendering.
- **Midjourney**: High-fidelity custom visual assets and conceptual photography.
- **Remotion**: Programmatic short-form video generation from text briefs.
- **ElevenLabs**: Human-quality voiceover generation.

### 5. Publishing & Social
- **Metricool**: Unified multi-channel scheduling, automatic optimal-time publishing.
- **WordPress**: Blog publishing, CMS formatting, and schema markup injection.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP)**: Local post updates and business announcements.
- **Meta Graph API (FB & IG), LinkedIn API, TikTok API**: Direct social media distribution.

### 6. Reviews & Reputation
- **Podium / NiceJob**: Automated review request sequences post-purchase.
- **Google Business Profile Reviews**: Direct review sentiment aggregation and response syncing.

### 7. Leads & Conversations
- **Omnichannel Chat Tile (IG DM, Messenger, WhatsApp)**: Real-time inbound lead ingestion.
- **HubSpot CRM**: Lead record creation, lifecycle staging, contact scoring.
- **Twilio**: SMS confirmation and alerts.
- **Calendly**: Frictionless meeting scheduling directly within chat flows.

### 8. Results & ROI
- **BigQuery / Postgres (pgvector)**: System of record and vector embeddings memory store.
- **Looker / Metabase**: Business intelligence dashboards, ROAS calculation, revenue attribution.
