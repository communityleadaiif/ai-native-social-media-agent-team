# Brand Brain Core

## Brand Identity & Values
- **Name**: Studio Skincare & Wellness
- **Core Mission**: Simplifying everyday routines with honest, science-backed skin clarity.
- **Brand Personality**: Friendly expert, empathetic, calm, straightforward.
- **Target Audience**: Working professionals and wellness-conscious individuals aged 24-45 looking for fuss-free, effective daily skin health.
- **Key Positioning**: No 12-step gimmicks. Just the essential 3 steps that work.
