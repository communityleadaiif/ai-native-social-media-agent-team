# Content Engine Playbook

## Production Stages
1. **Hook Formula**: Problem + Contrarian Truth + Immediate Promise of Simplicity.
2. **Body Structure**: 3 actionable takeaways with bullet points; short sentences (<15 words).
3. **CTA Style**: Low friction, conversational invite. Never aggressive urgency.
4. **Platform Formats**:
   - **Instagram**: High-contrast 4:5 carousels or aesthetic 9:16 Reels.
   - **LinkedIn**: Thought leadership breakdown of routine efficiency and ingredient transparency.
   - **TikTok**: Behind-the-scenes laboratory / formulation demonstration.
